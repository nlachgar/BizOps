01 / AI & automation

2025 – 2026 Rock Rabbit

Incentive verification, not manual combing

Rock Rabbit's analysts were qualifying incentives by hand — state by state, region by region, locality by locality — before a project could even be scoped. I built a scraping tool on Claude and n8n that turned that manual combing process into an incentive verification workflow, so analysts check and confirm rather than search from scratch.

01Analysts spend hours combing state, regional, and local incentive sources by hand.
02Claude and n8n scrape and structure that same incentive data automatically.
03Analysts shift to verification and to folding incentives into the product — the higher-value work.
04More incentives surface faster, which means more projects get funded and more revenue.
